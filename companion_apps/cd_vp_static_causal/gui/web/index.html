What this app does

Uses normalized phosphopeptide intensities from control and dDAVP-treated cells as separate observations. dDAVP, time, and their interaction are explicit; dDAVP enters the biological graph only through PKA (PRKACA). Every phosphosite remains a separate observed node.

Checking causal identification engine…

ID and IDC availability is verified locally before a run starts.

Explicit biological representation. The model uses latent Activity: protein nodes and observed protein phospho-site nodes. Until regulatory-site annotations are supplied, activity→site arrows are labeled measurement assumptions—not claims that those sites activate their proteins.
Joint experimental design. Positive TMT intensities are log2-transformed and median-centered within each channel. Control and dDAVP samples remain separate. Inference uses 12 matched time×replicate blocks, so the app does not pretend the 24 channels are 24 independent replicates.
Interpretation guardrail. Candidate scores combine overlapping local checks, so they rank compatibility only. They are not graph posterior probabilities or global p-values.
