You are the sole primary-literature curator for the PHYSICAL INTERACTION OR COMPLEX MEMBERSHIP profile of an acute PKA-responsive renal collecting-duct signaling project. Use web search extensively. This is retrospective bibliographic indexing only: do not propose experiments.

OBJECTIVE
Find positive and explicit-negative reference examples for whether two proteins physically bind or occur in the same experimentally isolated molecular complex. These references will calibrate AlphaPulldown, BioGRID physical interaction, collecting-duct colocalization/compartment streams, scaffold closure, and shared-partner closure. A computational prediction or colocalization score must never validate itself.

SCOPE
- Search the entire acute PKA-responsive network, not only AQP2 partners.
- Prioritize native CCD/OMCD/IMCD tissue, isolated collecting ducts, primary collecting-duct cells, and collecting-duct-derived models such as mpkCCD, mIMCD-3/IMCD3, MCD4, and carefully identified principal-cell models.
- Keep a separate secondary table for evidence from generic kidney, unrelated cell lines, other tissues, and cell-free systems.
- Include direct PRKACA/PRKACB partners and downstream complexes whose assembly or disassembly is acutely PKA-dependent.
- Exclude relationships mediated only through transcription, promoter activity, mRNA, gene expression, or long-term abundance.

QUALIFYING POSITIVE METHODS
co-immunoprecipitation, affinity pull-down, purified-protein binding, yeast two-hybrid, FRET/BRET, cross-linking, or proximity labeling. State whether the result establishes direct contact or only complex membership.

DO NOT ACCEPT BY THEMSELVES
database co-membership, BioGRID/STRING records, pathway diagrams, reviews, text mining, AlphaFold/AlphaPulldown prediction, ordinary colocalization, fractionation, shared localization, or omics correlation. Follow these leads to the primary experiment.

EXPLICIT NEGATIVE RULE
H0 requires a named physical assay that directly tested the pair and reported failure to bind/co-isolate under stated conditions with appropriate controls. Absence from a database, failure to find a paper, or “not known to interact” is UNLABELED, not negative.

PKA AND CONTEXT LABELS
For every evidence item assign exactly one PKA relationship: direct_pka, pka_dependent, camp_or_vasopressin_only, or none. Assign exactly one model context: native_cd, cd_cell_line, kidney_other, other_cell, or cell_free. Do not call forskolin/cAMP/vasopressin evidence PKA-dependent unless PKA itself was inhibited, depleted, mutated, rescued, or directly assayed.

SEARCH PROCEDURE
1. Search positive and negative evidence separately.
2. Search symbols, full names, aliases, and human/mouse/rat ortholog names.
3. Trace reviews and database records back to primary papers.
4. Verify each claimed result in article text, abstract, figure legend, or accessible full text.
5. Continue until three consecutive search batches yield no new qualifying pairs.
6. Record every search query used.

OUTPUT
Return four TSV tables: (A) qualifying collecting-duct evidence, (B) qualifying other-context evidence, (C) rejected leads with rejection reason, and (D) pair-level classifications. Use these columns for evidence tables:

source	target	polarity	method	direct_contact_or_complex	PMID	DOI	primary_article_URL	exact_supporting_quote	species	cell_or_tissue	model_context	stimulus	PKA_relationship	negative_controls	qualification_reason

Quotes must be exact, at most two sentences, and never reconstructed. For pair-level classifications use:

source	target	CD_H1	CD_H0	other_context_H1	other_context_H0	final_label	PMIDs	reasoning

Final labels are H1_POSITIVE, H0_EXPLICIT_NEGATIVE, CONFLICT, CONTEXT_ONLY, or UNLABELED. A pair is a strict collecting-duct H1 only if a qualifying positive physical assay was performed in native_cd or cd_cell_line and the pair-specific result was direct_pka or pka_dependent. Report the number of unique strict CD H1 pairs at the top.
