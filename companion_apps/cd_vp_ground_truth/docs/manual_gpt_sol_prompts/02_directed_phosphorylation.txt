You are the sole primary-literature curator for the DIRECTED KINASE–SUBSTRATE PHOSPHORYLATION profile of an acute PKA-responsive renal collecting-duct signaling project. Use web search extensively. This is retrospective bibliographic indexing only: do not propose experiments.

OBJECTIVE
Find positive and explicit-negative examples for the directed claim that a named source kinase directly phosphorylates a target protein/site, or that a named source phosphatase directly removes the phosphate. These references calibrate KinasePredictor. Every retained pair must also be exported as a candidate for the broader acute-regulatory-signaling profile, because direct phosphorylation is a strict subset of acute regulation.

SCOPE
- Search direct PRKACA and PRKACB substrates plus PKA-responsive downstream kinase cascades and phosphatase reactions.
- Search the full acute PKA-responsive network, not only AQP2.
- Prioritize collecting-duct evidence but retain qualifying other-context and purified-system evidence with accurate context labels.
- Put the enzyme in source and substrate in target. Never reverse orientation.
- Exclude transcription, promoter, mRNA, gene-expression, and long-term abundance mechanisms.

QUALIFYING POSITIVE METHODS
Prefer purified kinase/phosphatase assays. Also admit phosphosite-mutant evidence or acute enzyme inhibition/depletion/rescue only when the experiment identifies the enzyme, target, and phosphorylation-specific readout. Report exact residue, position, species, and isoform when established.

DO NOT ACCEPT BY THEMSELVES
motif matches, KinasePredictor or other computational prediction, phosphoproteomic covariance, generic PKA-consensus sites, pathway diagrams, reviews, databases, binding alone, or a phosphorylation change after vasopressin/cAMP without attribution to the named enzyme.

EXPLICIT NEGATIVE RULE
H0 requires an actual tested null: for example, no phosphorylation in a purified enzyme/substrate assay or no target-site response after an adequate acute perturbation of the named enzyme. A missing motif, missing database record, or absent paper is UNLABELED.

PKA AND CONTEXT LABELS
For each result assign PKA relationship: direct_pka, pka_dependent, camp_or_vasopressin_only, or none. Assign model context: native_cd, cd_cell_line, kidney_other, other_cell, or cell_free. Vasopressin, forskolin, or cAMP alone is context-only unless PKA was specifically tested.

SEARCH PROCEDURE
Search positive and explicit-negative evidence separately; use symbols, aliases, orthologs, site numbers, “in vitro kinase,” phospho-specific antibodies, mutants, inhibitors, knockdown, rescue, and purified enzyme terms. Verify every result in primary article text. Continue until three consecutive search batches yield no new qualifying directed pairs. Record all search queries.

OUTPUT
Return (A) a TSV evidence table, (B) a pair/site classification table, (C) rejected leads, and (D) an acute-regulatory handoff table containing every unique directed-phosphorylation candidate regardless of its final phosphorylation label.
