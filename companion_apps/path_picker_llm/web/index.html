      What “grounded” means here
      
    
      A proposed factual claim must pass source retrieval, quotation matching, and independent entailment checks. Failed checks stay visible but cannot support the final paragraph. Computational BFs, published facts, and mechanistic inferences remain distinct. This reduces hallucination risk; it does not guarantee correctness or exhaustive literature coverage. Inspect the sources before choosing a lab experiment.
